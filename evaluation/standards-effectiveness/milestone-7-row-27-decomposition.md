`workflows/implementation.md` is the sole normative owner for selecting change
description evidence from affected risk, contracts, decisions, alternatives,
behavior, migration, operations, and Verification claims. The pull-request
template is an optional derived projection; it cannot require a pull request,
provider, fixed headings, checklist completion, or duplicated durable rationale.

links selected owned evidence without transferring authority. Missing selected
evidence is `unavailable`; contradictory projection is `invalid`; unsupported
transport is `unsupported` and does not authorize omission or substitution.
