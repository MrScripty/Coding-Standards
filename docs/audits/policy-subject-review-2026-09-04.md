# Policy subject review

A policy subject earns a separate review boundary when it owns a distinct,
material coding or Engine guarantee. A heading, procedural checklist, repeated
specialization, or historical test fixture is not enough. The review keeps
24 subjects and unregisters 27. The complete per-subject decisions and rationale
are in [the review inventory](policy-subject-review-2026-09-04.json).

Keep design ownership, immutable replay, supported contract semantics, versions,
identity and validation boundaries, filesystem safety, actual dependency use,
generated-contract routing, proportionate planning, actual plan records,
projection updates, evidence quality, and publication/resource safety.

Remove the separate certificates for planning and Git administrative substeps,
unsupported Git operations, repeated generated-contract policy, and detailed
oracle techniques already governed by the retained evidence boundary.

This is registration pruning. Useful normative Markdown, heading links, routing,
and module-level navigation remain intact. Unregistered sections remain subject
to ordinary standards review; changes outside exact registered units still use
the Engine's conservative whole-artifact analysis. Unregistration is not a
certificate of compliance or a waiver of the guidance.

The Engine maintenance operation accepts explicit subject IDs, removes their
registrations and incident policy relationships, drops empty declaration sources,
and prunes certificates invalidated by the final candidate. It preserves all
standard text and retained subjects. It rejects unknown IDs rather than silently
turning a misspelling into a no-op. Historical snapshots retain their original
registrations. No replacement certificate or tombstone claims that the underlying
coding guidance was retired.
