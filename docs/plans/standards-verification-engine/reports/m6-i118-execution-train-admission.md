## Owned Contract

The typed check will consume the immutable execution train, its decomposition,
the generated owner map, and exact accepted dispositions. It must preserve:

- exact ordered baseline rows, ID ranges, waves, sources, owners, activation,
  checkpoints, and immutable totals;
- exact decomposition coverage and child ordering without duplicate or
  out-of-range identifiers;
- one-time owner creation, completion-sensitive effective owner state, and
  contained owner-path existence;

  and one contiguous active frontier; and
- exact remaining-ID coverage against the owner map.

The check is migration-only. Its module, check-kind registration, suite,
registry entry, and direct test receive explicit `zero-bash-accepted` terminal
dispositions. No Bash execution, embedded callback, compatibility parser,
copied progress snapshot, or fallback is admitted.
