unit. Engine composition resolved every claim against its current semantic
contract and produced exact requirement/certificate subject equality. The
static Verifier consumed that same Analysis-owned result.

Focused regression evidence proves that a representation-only raw-byte change:

1. changes the exact horizon, requirement, attestation, and certificate
   identities;
2. leaves the authored claim bytes unchanged; and
3. still produces current authorized coverage.

Incompatible subject semantic revision, horizon provider/version,
relationship-kind contract version, applicability language version, or
coverage-evidence contract does not auto-apply. Generated requirement handles
are rejected in authored claim sources.
