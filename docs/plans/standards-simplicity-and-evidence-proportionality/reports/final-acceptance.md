# Standards Simplicity And Evidence Proportionality Final Acceptance

## Accepted Outcome

The standards now apply simplicity to the composed artifact, admit permanent
evidence from reachable risk and marginal deciding value, preserve proof only
for its valid representation and boundary lifetime, scale closure and version
machinery to actual promises, compare established dependencies before owning
standardized behavior locally, and stop systemic correction at a dispositioned
semantic owner and reachable consumer population.

The revisions deepen existing standards and conformance suites. They add no
adopter enforcement harness, no A1c rule, no A1c implementation change, and no
parallel policy owner for concepts already owned by Core, Architecture,
Contracts, Dependencies, Planning, or Verification.
