# Milestone 3 Coverage Identity Replan

## Trigger

Repository-local coverage attestations are complete analysis inputs. If a
coverage requirement includes the complete analysis snapshot in its identity,
committing the attestation necessarily changes that identity and invalidates
the attestation. The legacy audit horizon also uses current policy-impact
declarations as its only membership boundary, so it cannot reveal a consumer
omitted from both declarations and the policy-impact node catalog.

## Accepted Design

Use two immutable identities:

1. `AnalysisSnapshot` binds the complete analysis input closure, including
   attestations. It governs packet and report reproducibility.
2. `CoverageAuthorityView` binds only typed inputs capable of changing
   consumer-discovery coverage. It governs requirement and certificate reuse.

A requirement records its source snapshot only as provenance. Its identity is
derived from the coverage view and exact coverage subject. An attestation
references that requirement. Committing the attestation stales the old packet,
but a new analysis derives the same view and requirement and can generate a
certificate from the attestation.

Coverage identity binds semantic payload rather than the transient
`proposed`/`accepted` label. Promotion of identical policy content,
relationships, and semantic revision therefore preserves applicable coverage.

## Coverage Projection

The view includes:

- policy-unit ID, owner, target semantic revision, and content and structural
  digests;
- every compiled outgoing policy-impact relationship and its kind contract;
- relationship registry and provider contracts;
- applicability language, program dependencies, and referenced fact schema;
- canonical identity-resolution, coverage, attestation, authorization, and
  evidence-provider contracts; and
- the registered horizon provider and every resolved member with its relevant
  content, structural, or semantic fingerprint.

It excludes attestations and their source registrations, generated
certificates, packets, reports, timestamps, display summaries, non-semantic
storage locations, and change-specific dispositions. Exclusion is by typed
artifact role. A relationship declaration placed under an attestation path
remains relationship authority and enters the view.

## Audit Horizon

Register `audit-horizon.policy-impact-consumers` version 1. Its deterministic
provider derives typed members from independent authorities:

- canonical module and policy-unit corpora;
- the edge-source registry and registered graph-provider contracts;
- every suite in the suite registry;
- repository artifacts explicitly referenced by those registered suites,
  including prompts, templates, documentation, references, fixtures, and
  evidence inputs; and
- the policy-impact node catalog as supplemental canonical identity.

Current policy-impact declarations do not define horizon membership. Suite
inputs are discovered only through registered suite definitions, not by a
repository-wide query scan. Every member binds content or semantic state, so a
changed existing consumer can invalidate coverage without changing membership.

## Authority And Lifecycle

`CoverageAuditRequirement` is mechanically derived. A reviewer authors a
`CoverageAttestation` containing the requirement handle, complete conclusion,
evidence, exclusions, rationale, provenance, and schema version. Its identity
comes from canonical content rather than filename. A generated
`ConsumerCoverageCertificate` binds view, requirement, attestation, evidence,
and contract digests. Certificates are reproducible products, not authored
repository authority.

The completed report remains the sole owner of change-specific dispositions.
Completion proves exact equality between required coverage subjects and valid
certificate subjects, and between reached consumer obligations and valid
disposition obligations.

## Atomic Cutover

The replacement slice must:

1. Replace the v2 audit schema shapes with coverage-view, requirement,
   attestation, certificate, and exact completion shapes.
2. Add the horizon and attestation source authorities.
3. Implement coverage derivation, validation, and deterministic identities in
   `standards_analysis`.
4. Remove `audit_catalog`, compiler audit loading and matching,
   `PolicyImpactSemantics.audit_declaration`, and
   `CompiledPolicyImpactSet.audited_owners`.
5. Remove legacy module-level audit authority and make static verification
   consume the same analysis coverage interface.
6. Update Standards Engine inspection and all fixtures without a compatibility
   path.

Legacy module audits may be cited as migration evidence, but policy-unit
attestations require an explicit reviewed baseline and cannot be generated by
blind record conversion.

## Replan Triggers

Replan before implementation continues if the registered suite definitions do
not provide an independently useful consumer horizon, typed-role projection
cannot prevent authority from escaping by location, attestation bootstrap
requires unrecorded semantic judgment, or static verification cannot consume
the analysis coverage interface without reversing dependency direction.

## Next Slice

Implement and verify this atomic replacement. No packet resolution, controlled
authoring, evidence-oracle policy, external baseline, or verifier migration
package is admitted by this replan.
